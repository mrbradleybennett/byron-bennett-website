My Story

My music is a journey of my life.

I was a bit off the rails as a kid. I didn't fit into the box at high school.

Writing music was a way for me to process my own emotions. It was my own therapy. I was 16 when I started releasing music under experiment.

Some people loved it ... some didn't.
Some had my back ... some didn't.
That's high school.

When I finished school I surrounded myself with people who believed in me and started releasing music under my own name.

Wherever you are on your journey, I hope my music connects with you and helps you on your path.
